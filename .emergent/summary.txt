<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to upgrade the ERP system to support Indian GST-compliant billing and generate professional, standardized reports similar to platforms like Zoho Inventory, Tally, and Vyapar.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
1.  **GST Billing Engine:**
    *   Sellers must be able to configure their business GST details (GSTIN, State).
    *   Products must have configurable GST rates (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and HSN codes.
    *   Customer records must include their state and GSTIN.
    *   The system must automatically calculate taxes (CGST/SGST vs. IGST) based on a comparison between the seller's state and the buyer's state (Place of Supply). The seller should not manually select the tax type.
2.  **GST-Compliant Invoices:**
    *   The invoice creation UI must show columns for HSN, Taxable Amount, CGST, SGST, IGST, and Total.
    *   The generated PDF invoice must be GST-compliant, including all necessary business/customer details and a full tax breakdown.
3.  **Standardized & GST Reports:**
    *   Create a GST Summary Report (similar to GSTR-1) showing taxable amounts and tax totals grouped by GST rate.
    *   Standardize existing reports (Sales, Inventory) and add new ones (Profit, Low Stock, Stock Movement) with professional layouts and relevant data columns.
4.  **Pending Orders (Backorder) System:**
    *   During invoice creation, if the requested quantity exceeds available stock, the system should allow for partial fulfillment.
    *   The remaining quantity should be automatically placed into a Pending Order.
    *   A new Pending Orders dashboard should be created for sellers to manage and fulfill these backorders when stock is replenished.
    *   Inventory display should be updated to show Total Stock, Reserved Stock (for pending orders), and Available Stock.
